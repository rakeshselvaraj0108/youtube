## Scope

Content intended to disgust or disturb: gore divorced from narrative purpose, bodily fluids, graphic medical procedure, and graphic treatment of animals. The distinguishing question is whether the shock is the point.

## Fully monetized when

- Mild shocking content inside an educational or documentary frame
- Unsensational food preparation involving animals

## Limited ads when

- Graphic human or animal body parts shown unobscured but with context
- Detailed medical procedure

## No ads when

- Gore or bodily harm presented for its own sake
- Graphic mistreatment of animals

## Documented exemptions

- Educational, documentary, scientific or artistic (EDSA) framing where the context is evident from the content itself rather than asserted in metadata
- News reporting on a matter of public interest
- Non-graphic verbal reference without depiction
- Clearly fictional or scripted context, signposted as such
- Quotation of a third party, particularly where it is also condemned
- Scientific or medical instruction where the graphic element is the subject being taught

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- vs AF-02 (violence): AF-04 needs no violent act. If a person caused the harm to another person, it is AF-02.
- vs AF-09 (controversial issues): AF-04 is visual. AF-09 covers the same subject matter discussed rather than shown.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: BLUR_REGION
- Typical span: Usually short, 1-5s, and often a single shot.
