## Scope

Firearms and firearm-adjacent content: handling, demonstration, modification, sale, and the environment in which any of it happens. Whether the setting is controlled is the pivotal fact.

## Fully monetized when

- Non-automatic and semi-automatic firearms handled in a controlled environment such as a supervised range
- Repair, maintenance and cleaning
- Responsible airsoft or replica use
- Discussion of firearms legislation

## Limited ads when

- Firearms used outside a controlled environment
- Detailed discussion of capability or lethality

## No ads when

- Instructions for manufacturing a firearm or converting one to fire automatically
- Facilitating a sale or transfer
- Fully automatic weapons
- Minors handling firearms without evident supervision

## Documented exemptions

- Educational, documentary, scientific or artistic (EDSA) framing where the context is evident from the content itself rather than asserted in metadata
- News reporting on a matter of public interest
- Non-graphic verbal reference without depiction
- Clearly fictional or scripted context, signposted as such
- Quotation of a third party, particularly where it is also condemned
- Legislative, historical and policy discussion
- Safety instruction conducted in a controlled setting

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- vs AF-02 (violence): presence and handling are AF-08. A firearm used against a person is AF-02.
- vs AF-11 (dishonest behaviour): manufacturing instructions are AF-08 specifically; AF-11 covers circumvention of other systems.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: MUTE
- Typical span: Variable, often the whole segment. Consider REFRAME.
