## Scope

A specific, identifiable tragedy, disaster, death or violent incident affecting real people. The question is whether the content engages with the event or exploits it for attention.

## Fully monetized when

- Discussion of loss or tragedy that is not exploitative
- Memorial and tribute content
- Factual reference with surrounding context

## Limited ads when

- Casualty figures stated without framing
- Extended discussion of a recent event

## No ads when

- Content exploiting an event for traffic, including keyword-stuffed titles referencing a tragedy
- Graphic footage of the event

## Documented exemptions

- Educational, documentary, scientific or artistic (EDSA) framing where the context is evident from the content itself rather than asserted in metadata
- News reporting on a matter of public interest
- Non-graphic verbal reference without depiction
- Clearly fictional or scripted context, signposted as such
- Quotation of a third party, particularly where it is also condemned
- Journalism and factual reporting on the event
- Commemoration, memorial and tribute
- First-hand testimony from those affected

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- vs AF-09 (controversial issues): AF-10 needs a named or otherwise identifiable event. Discussing suicide as a subject is AF-09; discussing a specific person's death is AF-10.
- vs AF-02 (violence): AF-10 covers the discussion. Footage of the event itself is AF-02 or AF-04.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: MUTE
- Typical span: Usually one or two sentences, 4-12s.
