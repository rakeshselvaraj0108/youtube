## Scope

Whether a long video carries chapter markers. Navigation aid; matters more the longer the runtime.

## Fully monetized when

- Chapters present, or a runtime short enough not to need them

## Limited ads when

- Long runtime with no chapter markers

## No ads when



## Documented exemptions

- Deliberate artistic choice where the content itself makes the intent evident rather than the metadata asserting it
- A measurement taken over a span too short to characterise the file

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- Advisory. Absence of chapters is never a monetization risk and must never be scored as one.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: NONE
- Typical span: File-scoped.
