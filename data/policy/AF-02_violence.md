## Scope

Real or dramatised physical violence, injury, blood and its aftermath. What matters is whether the injury is the subject of the shot and whether the content dwells on it — not whether it appears at all. Gameplay violence is treated differently from real footage.

## Fully monetized when

- Violence implied rather than shown
- Unedited gameplay violence outside the opening seconds
- Mild violence with minimal blood, not held in focus
- Law enforcement or emergency response footage in a news frame

## Limited ads when

- Real injury shown briefly with visible blood
- Bodies with visible injury in an educational or news setting
- Graphic game violence in the thumbnail or the first fifteen seconds

## No ads when

- Graphic real injury, mutilation or death held in focus
- Violence glorified or presented approvingly
- Graphic footage produced by or promoting a violent organisation

## Documented exemptions

- Educational, documentary, scientific or artistic (EDSA) framing where the context is evident from the content itself rather than asserted in metadata
- News reporting on a matter of public interest
- Non-graphic verbal reference without depiction
- Clearly fictional or scripted context, signposted as such
- Quotation of a third party, particularly where it is also condemned

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- vs AF-04 (shocking content): AF-02 requires an act of violence. Gore with no violent act — a surgical procedure, an accident aftermath, decomposition — is AF-04.
- vs AF-08 (firearms): a firearm present is AF-08. A firearm used against a person is AF-02, and AF-02 governs.
- vs AF-09 (controversial issues): AF-02 is depiction. Discussion of abuse or self-harm without depiction is AF-09.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: BLUR_REGION
- Typical span: Typically 2-8s. Blur the region rather than cutting: cutting re-encodes and usually removes narrative the creator needs.
