## Scope

Tobacco, vaping and related products, and by extension other regulated goods such as alcohol where the same promotion logic applies. Incidental adult reference is treated far more leniently than promotion.

## Fully monetized when

- Incidental reference in content addressed to adults
- Cessation, harm-reduction and health-consequence content

## Limited ads when

- Consumption shown on camera without promotion
- Product review of a regulated good

## No ads when

- Promotion of tobacco or vaping products
- Content encouraging excessive consumption
- Any depiction inside content made for kids

## Documented exemptions

- Educational, documentary, scientific or artistic (EDSA) framing where the context is evident from the content itself rather than asserted in metadata
- News reporting on a matter of public interest
- Non-graphic verbal reference without depiction
- Clearly fictional or scripted context, signposted as such
- Quotation of a third party, particularly where it is also condemned
- Cessation support and public-health messaging

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- vs AF-07 (recreational drugs): legality is the divider. Tobacco, vaping and alcohol are AF-12 and start from a permissive baseline.
- vs AF-13 (kids and families): the permissive baseline disappears entirely if the content reads as made for children.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: NONE
- Typical span: Usually advisory; a passing mention rarely warrants an edit.
