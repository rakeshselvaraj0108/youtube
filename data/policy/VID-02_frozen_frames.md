## Scope

A sustained span with no motion between frames — a stalled screen recording, a render that silently dropped frames, or a capture device that disconnected mid-record while audio kept rolling.

## Fully monetized when

- No sustained freeze outside a deliberate still frame

## Limited ads when

- A freeze long enough to read as a technical fault

## No ads when

- Extended freeze — the upload is functionally broken for its duration

## Documented exemptions

- Deliberate artistic choice where the content itself makes the intent evident rather than the metadata asserting it
- A measurement taken over a span too short to characterise the file

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- A deliberate still frame under narration is short and usually intentional; this clause is scoped to freezes long enough that a viewer checks whether playback stalled.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: NONE
- Typical span: The frozen span itself.
