## Scope

A sustained span with no picture — a black gap from a bad edit, a missing clip, or a render that failed to composite.

## Fully monetized when

- No sustained black span outside a deliberate transition

## Limited ads when

- A black span long enough to read as a mistake

## No ads when

- Extended black where content was clearly intended

## Documented exemptions

- Deliberate artistic choice where the content itself makes the intent evident rather than the metadata asserting it
- A measurement taken over a span too short to characterise the file

## Signals that distinguish this clause from neighbours

- vs AUD-03 (dead air): this is the visual sibling of the same class of editing accident, measured on the picture rather than the sound.

## Remediation guidance

- Preferred fix: NONE
- Typical span: The black span itself.
